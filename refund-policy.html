Cancellation & Refund Policy

Please read the cancellation rules before paying for your accommodation.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. When the cancellation time is measured

The 72-hour period begins at the exact date and time your booking becomes CONFIRMED by HANBOK VILLA, after the required payment has been successfully verified. It does not begin when you submit a booking request.

All dates and cancellation cut-offs in this policy are measured in Sri Lanka local time (Asia/Colombo, UTC+05:30), using the check-in date stated in the confirmed reservation.

2. Guest cancellation & refund schedule

Important — late cancellation takes priority: If you cancel on the calendar day before your scheduled check-in date, on your scheduled check-in date, or after check-in, no refund (0%) is provided, even if the booking was confirmed less than 72 hours earlier. This is subject to any mandatory rights under applicable law.
When the guest cancelsRefund of amount paid

Refund percentages apply to the amount actually paid for the affected accommodation booking. The no-refund period begins at 00:00 midnight in Sri Lanka on the calendar day before check-in, not 24 hours before your arrival time.

Example: If your check-in date is 20 November, then the 0% refund period begins at 00:00 on 19 November (Sri Lanka time). A qualifying cancellation before that cut-off receives 100% if made within 72 hours of booking confirmation, or 70% if made afterward. If HANBOK VILLA itself cancels because accommodation cannot be provided, the guest receives 100%.

3. How to submit a cancellation

Use the secure cancellation link provided with your confirmed booking, if available, or email hanbokhikka@gmail.com with your booking reference. The timestamp when HANBOK VILLA receives a valid cancellation request determines the applicable refund window.

4. Cancellations after arrival or failure to check in

Guests who cancel after their scheduled check-in date has begun are not eligible for a refund under the ordinary guest cancellation schedule. A no-show is also subject to the 0% refund rule. Where mandatory consumer rights apply, they will be respected.

5. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method where supported. Processing times depend on the payment provider and the card issuer or bank. If a 70% partial refund cannot be processed automatically by the payment provider, HANBOK VILLA will contact the guest to arrange the refund securely by an appropriate supported method.

Cancellation and refund completion are separate events. We will communicate the refund status. Please do not send full payment-card details by email or messaging apps.

6. Cancellation by HANBOK VILLA

If HANBOK VILLA cancels a confirmed reservation because it cannot provide the accommodation, the guest will receive a 100% refund of the amount paid for that booking, regardless of the 72-hour rule or how close the check-in date is.

7. Questions and legal rights

For cancellation or refund questions, contact hanbokhikka@gmail.com. Nothing in this policy limits mandatory rights under applicable law.
