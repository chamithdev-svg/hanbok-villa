1. About HANBOK VILLA

HANBOK VILLA provides holiday accommodation at Panvila Road, Hikkaduwa 80240, Sri Lanka. Guests may enquire about individual rooms or booking the entire villa. The property has two bedrooms and accommodates up to six guests in total. Larger-group requests are handled individually and are not automatically confirmed.

2. Reservations and confirmation

Submitting an enquiry or a booking request does not by itself guarantee availability. Availability, guest numbers, dates, accommodation type and the final price must be confirmed by HANBOK VILLA. Where an online payment option is offered, the reservation is confirmed only after the required payment has been successfully verified and a booking confirmation has been issued.

Please provide accurate contact details and booking information. Keep the confirmation email and booking reference for future correspondence.

3. Prices and payment

Current rates and the currency of payment will be shown during the booking process or communicated in the booking confirmation. Once integrated and activated, online reservations require full payment of the confirmed booking amount. Payment services may be provided by an independent payment gateway; its own service terms may also apply.

Do not send payment card numbers, security codes or passwords by email or WhatsApp. Online card payments, when available, should be completed through the authorised checkout flow.

4. Check-in, check-out and guests

Check-in and check-out arrangements will be communicated in the booking confirmation. Guests should notify the villa of expected arrival times and any changes. Occupancy must not exceed the confirmed guest count without prior approval.

Guests are expected to respect the property, neighbours, staff and local laws. Any confirmed damage or exceptional cleaning costs will be discussed with the guest and handled in accordance with applicable law.

Facilities and optional services are provided as described on our website or in the final booking confirmation, subject to availability. Transport, scooter or car rental, tours and other services may have additional conditions or charges. Any complimentary services must be explicitly confirmed in writing.

6. Cancellations and refunds

Our Cancellation & Refund Policy forms part of these Terms. For guest cancellations made before the calendar day preceding check-in, a 100% refund applies within 72 hours of booking confirmation and a 70% refund applies after 72 hours. However, guest cancellations on the day before check-in, on check-in day, or after check-in are non-refundable (0%), even if the reservation was confirmed within the previous 72 hours. Cancellation by HANBOK VILLA because accommodation cannot be provided remains eligible for a 100% refund. Time calculations use Sri Lanka local time. Please read the full policy before making payment.

7. Changes or cancellations by the villa

If HANBOK VILLA cannot honour a confirmed booking and cancels it, a 100% refund of the amount received for that booking will be arranged. We will notify the guest using their provided contact details.

We use booking information to process reservations, provide support and send important updates related to your stay. See our Privacy Policy for details.
