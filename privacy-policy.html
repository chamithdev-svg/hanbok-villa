Privacy Policy

How we handle information shared when you contact us or request accommodation.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Who we are

HANBOK VILLA is a holiday accommodation business located at Panvila Road, Hikkaduwa 80240, Sri Lanka. For privacy questions, contact hanbokhikka@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

When you send a booking request or communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and contact preferences.
  • Check-in and check-out dates, room or villa selection, number of guests and messages or special requests you provide.
  • Booking reference, confirmation, cancellation and payment-status records needed to manage reservations.
  • Basic technical and security information, such as IP address and anti-spam verification results, where necessary to operate or protect the site.

We do not ask guests to send card numbers, CVV codes or passwords through our booking forms. When online payments are enabled, card entry is handled by the payment service provider rather than by email or WhatsApp.

3. Why we use information

  • To respond to enquiries, check availability and administer bookings.
  • To send booking confirmations, changes, important service communications and cancellation updates.
  • To verify payment status, process refunds and investigate potential fraud or misuse.
  • To maintain service security, fulfil legal or recordkeeping obligations, and respond to legitimate requests.

4. Third-party service providers

We use service providers to host the website, store and manage booking requests, deliver emails and help protect forms from spam or abuse. When card payments are enabled, a payment gateway will process payments and related payment data under its own privacy terms. We share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant service.

5. Data storage and transfers

Service providers may process or store information outside Sri Lanka. We aim to use appropriate safeguards and contractual protections where required by applicable law. We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above and to meet applicable legal obligations; unnecessary data should be deleted or anonymised when no longer needed.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unauthorised access and protect booking information. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please contact us if you suspect a security or privacy issue involving your booking.

7. Cookies and local browser storage

Our website and service providers may use essential cookies or browser storage to support operation, security and user experience. Any optional analytics or advertising tools, if introduced, should be explained in an updated notice and used subject to applicable requirements.

8. Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by emailing hanbokhikka@gmail.com. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law, taking account of any legal retention requirements.

9. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy if our booking process, payment service providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.
